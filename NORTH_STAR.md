# Math Workshop

A local-first mathematics workbench for children with different reading levels
and learning needs. Make quantities visible, let learners change representations,
and connect their actions to truthful equations. Adults should be able to keep
material relevant without losing the meaning of earlier work.

The near-term product is short, enjoyable practice: count and combine small groups;
explore equal rows and factor pairs; later connect arrays to place value and area.
Help is available before failure. Fingers, retries and interruptions are welcome.
No timing pressure, ability labels, diagnosis or inferred mastery.

A learner's concepts and skill-specific needs guide learning, rather than rigid
age/grade placement or a global ability score. Standards may be reference tags;
they are not unlock gates, certification or a substitute for classroom evidence.
Learners should be free to explore ahead or voluntarily revisit an earlier idea.
This software should stay useful to families after the development experiment ends.

The owner's future navigation direction is a focused activity with a few relevant ways
to explore, backed by a network of concepts rather than one compulsory chain. This is
not implemented navigation.
[DESIGN](DESIGN.md#future-learning-navigation-not-implemented) owns the interaction
intention; [content guidance](.context/content.md#future-concept-relationships)
distinguishes supporting ideas from strict mathematical dependencies.

The repository should be understandable without a private conversation or paid AI
service. Code, structured content, decisions and tests form the durable record. Plans
must follow the implementation rather than compete with it.

This foundation supports independent addends 0–5 (answers 0–10) and configured factor
rectangles, defaulting to 24 tiles. Its purpose is to establish two small usable
experiences with real saves. Future work is ordered in [goals](goals/README.md).
Accounts, network pairing, multiplayer, broad curriculum authoring, runtime AI,
handwriting/photo grading, spelling, typing and offline iPad synchronization are outside
this foundation. The programming/data laboratory is a separate product.

The two demonstration learners are synthetic. Preserve quantity and identity,
short instructions, explicit help and recent submitted attempts without inferring
mastery. Mutable learner data stays separate from the embedded host executable.
Family identity, private conversations and classroom material are not source inputs.
