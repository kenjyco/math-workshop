## Example contract

For each goal, produce small Markdown examples with editable equations, lists or tables. Use an exact editable drawing such as SVG when spatial placement carries evidence. Supply a text equivalent giving the quantities, units and relationships. Identify schematic drawings and supply an actual scale or construction when asking for measurement. [Foundational example conventions](../examples/README.md)

Every example identifies its goal, content and concept IDs, grade-specific limits and source section. Specify the given quantities, the question, the reasoning between steps, and an answer with the requested meaning or units. Follow the relevant example with its short parent bridge. Several examples can share a bridge or diagram. Put the Practice reasoning into the example itself so a reader can follow it before inspecting the independent check. Concrete numbers and materials in the selected-evidence column are editorial choices. Preserve any representation, explanation or algorithm named in the grade expectation.

To accept a goal, locate evidence for every listed row, including parent statements and lettered parts. An independent reviewer must be able to reproduce the intermediate relationships, verify the diagram against its labels, and check the result by the stated independent method. For a group of examples covering several cases, identify which example satisfies each case. A fluency goal calls for accurate and efficient methods and the stated range of facts. Example review establishes content coverage. Learner fluency requires separate evidence.

For contrasting work, keep correct steps visible and identify the first discrepancy the record supports. Compare the original question, scratch calculation, answer field and any selected statement separately. Distinguish a missing step, a documented interruption, and a completed incorrect calculation. An unexplained wrong answer alone is insufficient to identify a cause.

The output of each goal ends with definitions in context, worked examples, representations, a concise bridge and mathematical verification. Full lessons, exercises delivered as activities, learner assessment, recursive help, saved work, feedback software and application or framework experiments are outside every goal.

## Using the relationships

Numbers order the authoring work. Each goal identifies the mathematical foundations or optional support for its particular interpretation, including earlier goals where helpful. The concept guide distinguishes necessary, helpful and sideways relationships across grades. Within a family, use the narrower topic named in a goal, such as completeness of a factor-pair search or the meaning of a zero quotient digit. [Concept guide](concepts.md)
