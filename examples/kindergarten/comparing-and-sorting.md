# A cup for each spoon

Kindergarten · G02 · Concepts `order`, `card`, `data` · Practice MP3. [G02 contract and standards, pp. 11–12](../../goals/kindergarten.md#g02-comparing-collections-and-sorting-by-count)

## Match across two groups

**K.CC.C.6.** Six spoons lie close together. Four cups stretch across a longer part of the table. Are there more cups or more spoons? Here each S stands for a spoon and each C for a cup.

```text
S S S S S S
C       C       C       C
```

Put one spoon in each cup. Four cup–spoon pairs use every cup. Two spoons still need cups. There are **more spoons than cups**, and **fewer cups than spoons**. Check by counting the spoons as four in cups and two on the table, six in all.

**Parent bridge.** “More” has a visible witness here. The two spoons left on the table have no partners, even though the row of cups stretched farther.

Bring two more cups and give each leftover spoon its own cup. There are six pairs and nothing left over. The two counts are **equal**. Matching works for collections of up to ten objects each in these Kindergarten comparisons.

## Read the numeral cards

**K.CC.C.7.** Compare written numerals from 1 through 10. The cards read **8** and **10**. Which number is less? In counting order we say 8, 9, 10. Read left to right, “eight is less than ten.” Switch the cards and the statement becomes “ten is greater than eight.” Two cards both marked **8** name the same number, so eight equals eight.

## Sort a handful by its markings

**K.MD.B.3.** Each tile is stamped with exactly one mark. Sort by that mark into the supplied categories star, moon and sun. Which categories occur least often? Here is the handful in the order it came out of the bag.

```text
moon  star  sun  star  moon  sun  star  sun  moon  star
```

A category's *frequency* is how many tiles belong to it. Move each tile once. Its position in the list lets us check where it went.

| Category | Positions in the handful | Number of tiles |
|---|---|---:|
| Moon | 1, 5, 9 | 3 |
| Sun | 3, 6, 8 | 3 |
| Star | 2, 4, 7, 10 | 4 |

From least to most frequent, **moon and sun tie at three each, then star has four**. Match the moons to the suns and neither group has a leftover. Match the stars to the suns and one star is left. All ten positions appear once, and 3 + 3 + 4 = 10. Kindergarten limits the count **in each category** to ten.
