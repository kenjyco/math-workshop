# What changes when a bundle is tied?

Grade 1 · G11 · Concepts `place`, `exchange`, `add` · MP7. Add within 100. For subtraction of exact tens, both operands are between 10 and 90 and the difference is at least zero. [Goal and standards, p. 16](../../goals/grade-1.md#g11-adding-and-subtracting-in-tens-and-ones)

## Three ways to add to a tray

**1.NBT.C.4.** A `T` is a bundle of ten sticks and an `i` is one loose stick. The two trays in each row are poured together. How many sticks are there now?

| Trays before | Sticks after combining | Written calculation and check |
|---|---|---|
| 32 + 5 · `TTT ii` and `iiiii` | Keep the three bundles. Two and five loose sticks give seven. | 32 + 5 = 30 + (2 + 5) = 37. Count on five from 32 to check, 33, 34, 35, 36, 37. |
| 32 + 20 · `TTT ii` and `TT` | Join three bundles and two bundles. The two loose sticks stay on the tray. | 32 + 20 = (30 + 20) + 2 = 52. Removing the two added bundles leaves 32. |
| 26 + 17 · `TT iiiiii` and `T iiiiiii` | Three bundles and thirteen loose sticks. Tie ten of the loose sticks, leaving three loose. | Tens give 20 + 10 = 30. Ones give 6 + 7 = 13. These **partial sums** combine as 30 + 13 = 30 + 10 + 3 = 43. |

Here is the exchange in the last row. Both lines describe the same sticks.

```text
Before tying    T T T     iiiiiiiiii iii
After tying     T T T T              iii
```

**Check the trade.** Untie the new bundle. Its ten sticks and the three still loose rebuild thirteen, exactly the ones subtotal. Count the finished tray another way, 10, 20, 30, 40, 41, 42, 43. Check against the original trays by moving four of the seventeen to the twenty-six. That makes thirty, with thirteen still to add.

**Parent bridge.** In a familiar written addition, 6 ones and 7 ones give 13 ones. Write the 3 in the ones column. The small 1 recorded above the tens column stands for the newly tied bundle. The tens column now has 2 + 1 + 1 = 4 tens.

## Ten more and ten less in your head

**1.NBT.C.5.** Start at 56. Imagine five bundles and six loose sticks. Adding a bundle keeps all six loose sticks and changes five tens to six. **Ten more is 66.** Taking one bundle from the original five leaves four tens and six ones. **Ten less is 46.** Undo each change to check. From 66 remove ten to get 56. From 46 add ten to get 56.

## Taking away whole bundles

**1.NBT.C.6.** Each removed bundle takes away ten sticks. The three columns show the starting tray, the action, and what the subtraction records.

| Start | Remove and inspect the rest | Written difference and check |
|---|---|---|
| 90 · `TTTTTTTTT` | Lift away `TTT`. Six bundles `TTTTTT` remain. | 9 tens − 3 tens = 6 tens, so **90 − 30 = 60**. Put three back and count 70, 80, 90. |
| 10 · `T` | Lift away the only bundle. Both the bundle space and the loose-stick space are empty. | 1 ten − 1 ten = 0 tens, so **10 − 10 = 0**. Put one ten back to recover 10. |

**Parent bridge.** In a written 90 − 30, the 9 and 3 share the tens column because both count whole bundles. The 6 there records sixty sticks. The ones column records zero loose sticks throughout.
